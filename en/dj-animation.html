DJ Animation

An entertainer DJ who gets every generation dancing: dance parties, weddings, birthdays and family celebrations.

Dance parties Weddings Birthdays Parties & associations

For a dance party, a wedding, a birthday or a community celebration, SLP offers an all-round entertainer DJ: someone who reads the room and gets everyone dancing, from the kids to the grandparents.

On the menu: hits from every era — the 80s, French pop, disco, dance, rock, Latin, plus all the dancefloor essentials — and a playlist built with you beforehand: your favourite songs, your key moments, and the list of tracks to avoid.

A microphone for speeches and announcements, support for the highlights (the couple's entrance, the cake, the first dance) and sound and lighting setup included: SLP takes care of all the technical side so you can enjoy the evening.
