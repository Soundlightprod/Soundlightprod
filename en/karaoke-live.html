The concept

A karaoke without a backing track: the band plays for you

Live karaoke replaces the fixed backing track with a band on stage. Every participant sings as if in a concert: the musicians kick off the song, adapt the key and tempo, support the voice, and the audience sings along thanks to the lyrics shown on a big screen.

A new concept, unique in France: karaoke with a real band, combined with an on-site sign-up kiosk and a dedicated app to manage sign-ups, the queue and the turns on stage.

For the organiser, the event stays simple to run. Singers sign up from their smartphone or on site, the running order is managed automatically and everyone is notified when their turn comes up: less waiting, less to organise.

SLP selects the set-up and coordinates everything: band, sound system, lighting, screen and installation, with a team on site.

Frequently asked questions

Everything about live karaoke

Is it a fully live karaoke?

Yes. Singers are accompanied by a band on stage, with no pre-recorded backing track. The band can adapt the key, the tempo or the length of a solo to put each participant at ease.

How do participants choose their song?

They browse the available repertoire, then sign up on site or from their smartphone by scanning a QR code. Their turn is managed automatically and they are notified when it is getting close.

Are sound, lighting and screens included?

Yes, in the turnkey package: sound system, lighting, screen, installation and on-site technical support. The set-up can also be integrated into equipment already present in your hall or at your festival.

What kinds of events is this format suited to?

Company parties and team building, town festivals, festivals, association events, bars and restaurants, private parties. The format adapts to the venue, the audience and the size of the crowd.

Can the event be customised with our colours?

Yes. Screens and visuals can carry your logo and identity. Additional options such as video capture or a mobile stage are available on request.

Where do you travel?

Anywhere in France. Tell us your venue and date: we check availability and reply quickly.

How many musicians are on stage?

The band has six musicians, supported by four technicians for sound, lighting, video and the control desk.

What if a singer is nervous or slips up?

The band is there to support them: it can restart a chorus, adapt the ending, change key or adjust the tempo live.

How many songs are available?

The repertoire has more than 180 songs, sorted by artist in a catalogue you can download from this page.

Can we run votes or a ranking?

Yes, for corporate events: votes, teams and rankings are possible, turning the evening into a group challenge.

Does it work outdoors?

Yes, the format adapts to indoor and outdoor stages: village hall, public square, marquee, festival.

What should we tell you to get a quote?

The date, venue, time, type of event and expected number of participants. We then propose a package suited to your venue and audience.
