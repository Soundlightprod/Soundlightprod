Technical services

Technical services

Sound and lighting designed for your venue, your audience and the atmosphere you want to create — set up and run by us, from the first line check to the last encore. Indoor or outdoor concerts, shows, conventions, weddings, community events: we adapt our setup to every event format.

What we do

Full technical production, from A to Z

Sound

Front-of-house PA, stage monitors and a digital console mixed live, matched to the audience size and the venue's acoustics.

Stage lighting

Moving heads, washes, haze effects and a DMX control desk for an atmosphere that follows the rhythm of your event.

Structure & rigging

Truss, stands and safe lifting to support lighting and speakers by the book.

Pyrotechnics

Fireworks displays and custom stage effects, carried out safely with all the required permits.

Technical crew

A technician on site on the day to run sound and lighting live, from soundcheck to load-out.

Advice & planning

A technical site visit and a stage plot tailored to your venue, well before the day of the event.

Every event format

Concerts

Shows & theatre

Companies, troupes and performances: discreet, precise operation in service of the staging.

Conventions & seminars

Weddings & receptions

Community festivals & public events

Corporate evenings & launches
