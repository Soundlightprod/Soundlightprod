The concept

The headline show for 50 years of disco

2027 will be no ordinary year: the film Saturday Night Fever turns 50. Its soundtrack remained one of the best-selling albums ever, until Thriller came along, and it brought disco to dance floors everywhere.

To celebrate, SLP offers a headline show unique in France devoted to the golden age of disco: the best of the genre, played by 100 % live musicians, with three first-of-their-kind tributes together on one stage.

A festive show for all ages: the hits everyone knows, the energy of live music and the atmosphere of the great disco nights.

A flexible format: 1, 2 or 3 tributes on stage, over 1 or 2 days, with a 100 % disco DJ to close the night.

FAQ

Everything about the 100 % disco show

Why a disco show in 2027?

Because 2027 marks 50 years of the film Saturday Night Fever and of the golden age of disco. This headline show celebrates it, with three live tributes on the same stage.

Are the musicians really live?

Yes. The show is played 100% by musicians on stage, with no pre-recorded backing track.

Can the night continue after the show?

Yes: a 100% disco DJ takes over to close the night, joined on request by a live percussionist. The show can also be played with 1, 2 or 3 tributes, over 1 or 2 days.

Where is the show available?

In France and Europe, for towns, festivals, companies, associations and private parties. Request a quote with the date and venue of your event.
